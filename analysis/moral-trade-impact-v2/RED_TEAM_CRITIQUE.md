# Red-Team Critique

## Reasons the model could overstate impact

- The stipulated active-user trajectory is conditional, not forecast adoption. “Meaningful active” may be easier to count than genuine causal participation.
- Prospective budgets may be aspirational, zero-spend mass may be larger, and repeated users may dominate apparent volume.
- A completed transfer or action is factual completion, not proof it would not have happened otherwise. Twelve-month displacement can remain too short.
- Redirect creators may not have credible frozen fallback plans. Structured terms reduce but do not eliminate strategic/perverse baselines.
- Opposed views can be resource-incompatible, threaten one another, or face bargaining/lock-in/power failures that simple cause-profile fit misses.
- Direct markets can unravel when WTA, WTP, evidence burden, timing, or role supply is correlated in worse ways than the factor model represents.
- Co-Act groups may exhibit correlated attrition; the aggregate role-completion prior may understate cascading failures in large groups.
- Voluntary DACs remain vulnerable to pivotality and free-riding. Failure bonuses do not remove the incentive to let others fund a successful public good.
- Pool-level heterogeneity is reduced-form. Smooth expected successes can hide discrete tail failures and correlated project quality.
- Cash costs omit unpaid burden by owner decision and may omit future paid legal/security/review work. This is a scope choice, not a claim those burdens are zero.
- Field allocations can look precise because they sum exactly while their causal and preference evidence remains weak.

## Reasons the model could understate impact

- It excludes Donation Upgrades, career/salary-gap pools, institutional trades, and Threshold Sign-Ons from central totals.
- It does not monetize labor, participant welfare, learning spillovers, network formation, or long-run institution effects.
- Causal action duration and complementary gains may persist beyond the modeled annual accounting window.
- Many-to-many clearing and repeat interaction could create thicker markets than central priors assume.
- Rescue classification deliberately excludes within-high-impact reallocation even where value differences could be large.

## Failure conditions for use

Do not use the central median as a report headline without owner ledger review, independent accounting audit, appropriate rounding, AI-assistance disclosure, and explicit cutover approval. Do not infer individual causal credit, field-level cost effectiveness, mechanism liveness, legal acceptability, or payment readiness. Do not average structural scenarios without independently justified probabilities.
