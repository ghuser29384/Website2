# Empirical Validation Plan

The next empirical study should estimate an intention-to-treat effect rather than “validate” a completed transaction as causal.

## Precise next experiment

Run a preregistered, prospective, graph-cluster randomized encouragement study only after an independent privacy/legal/product gate authorizes it. This analysis package does not launch the study.

1. Define eligible interaction-graph clusters before exposure; keep connected users in one assignment unit to limit cross-arm marketplace interference.
2. Before randomization, elicit frozen 12-month donation plans, action/time plans, accepted cause/resource profiles, and confidence bands. Record no payment credentials in the research artifact.
3. Randomize clusters in a 2x2 design: structured Moral Trade discovery/offer encouragement versus neutral availability, crossed with enhanced evidence/review support versus standard evidence support. Preserve ordinary product choice; the randomized object is encouragement/support, not compelled action.
4. Primary endpoint at 12 months: cluster-level `new cash + rescued cash - displacement - cash losses/costs`, with rescued and within-high-impact reallocation reported separately. Use assignment-policy ITT; never assign exact participant causal credit.
5. Secondary endpoints: 90-day early cash, additional ordinary/skilled hours, verified completion, support/review hours, trust, attrition, adverse events, and spillovers. Personal-income transfers remain separate.
6. Estimate effects with cluster-robust uncertainty, prespecified missing-data bounds, treatment-arm balance, exposure take-up, and interference sensitivity. Report both ITT and clearly labeled treatment-on-treated bounds only if the exclusion assumptions are defensible.
7. Follow all clusters at 90 days and 12 months, including non-completers, so brought-forward giving and displacement are measurable.

The current-main study candidate's 3,200-cluster figure is a synthetic planning envelope, not a powered sample-size conclusion. Recompute power from a blinded pilot variance estimate before launch.

## Mechanism calibration sequence

- Redirects: compare frozen planned fallbacks to cleared, settled, and 12-month realized destinations; estimate compatibility and displacement by source-side strata.
- Direct/Co-Acts: retain posted WTP/WTA, compatible supply/demand, evidence burden, role fill, attrition, completion, and duration; randomize discovery/evidence support where safe.
- DACs: begin with a no-money/shadow or explicitly authorized pledge-only pilot. Later, if separately approved, randomize published bonus curves or exploit preregistered surcharge-policy discontinuities; measure arrival, free-riding, success, reserve waiting, and user retention. Never expose an uncovered bonus promise.

Update the ledger from blinded or preregistered estimates. Preserve the frozen first run and log every post-result change with before/after output.
