# Accessibility and Mobile Test Checklist

Run this before calling the dashboard, agreement, payment, and first-visit interview flows production-ready.

## Keyboard

- Open and close the first-visit interview with keyboard only.
- Rank cause areas with buttons when drag and drop is not available.
- Submit payment, schedule, event, report, and rating forms without a mouse.
- Confirm focus is visible on header links, primary buttons, text links, inputs, and selects.

## Screen reader

- Confirm form labels are announced on signup, login, offer creation, dashboard, agreement detail, and admin pages.
- Confirm status banners announce errors and success states in reading order.
- Confirm repeated agreement/payment cards have distinguishable headings.

## Mobile

- Test the homepage opening sequence at 320px, 390px, 768px, and desktop widths.
- Test first-visit interview ranking and custom cause entry on mobile Safari and Chrome.
- Test dashboard cards, agreement detail forms, and admin queues on a real phone.
- Confirm no horizontal overflow on long moral-trade definition text.

## Reduced motion and contrast

- Confirm the site is usable with reduced motion enabled.
- Confirm button text and muted metadata meet contrast expectations on white backgrounds.
- Confirm disabled buttons are visibly disabled but still legible.
